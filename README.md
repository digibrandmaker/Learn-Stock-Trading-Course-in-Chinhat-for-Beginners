## Important Skills for Beginners

Anyone starting their stock market learning journey should focus on a few essential areas:

- **Market Fundamentals:** Understand stock exchanges, shares, and trading accounts.
- **Technical Analysis:** Learn how to read charts and identify common patterns.
- **Risk Management:** Study position sizing, stop-loss concepts, and capital protection.
- **Trading Psychology:** Understand the role of patience, discipline, and emotional control.
- **Research Skills:** Learn to evaluate information instead of relying on rumours or unverified tips.

These skills require consistent learning and practice. No course or strategy can guarantee profits in the stock market.
